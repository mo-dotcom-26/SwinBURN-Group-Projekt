Marketing Generalist

Reference number: MKT01

Mark Ritson, an outspoken global marketing professor, consultant, and columnist known for championing evidence-based strategy and branding principles, "A generalist understands that tactics without strategy is the noise before defeat, but strategy without execution is just a daydream. Our job is to bridge the gap between business reality and creative magic."

About the role:
We are hiring a Marketing Generalist to own our day-to-day marketing execution and help shape our strategy, working with Sales and IT so that what we promote matches what we can deliver. You will report to the Marketing Manager / Head of Marketing (or Head of Growth or COO if none yet), with a dotted line to Sales and the UX / Technical team.

What you will do:

  1. Plan and run campaigns across [channels, e.g. email, content, social, events] against agreed targets
  2. Turn product and technical information from IT into clear messaging for customers
  3. Work with UX and engineering to turn new site features, redesigned product pages, and improved application workflows into clear customer-facing messaging
  4. Use customer behavior data (conversion rates, drop-off points, search terms) to suggest improvements to product listings and key user journeys
  5. Partner with Sales on lead quality, handoff, and feedback on what is converting
  6. Run A/B tests with the UX team and report what the results mean for marketing
  7. Track performance in [tools, e.g. Google Analytics, CRM] and report monthly on [metrics, e.g. conversion rate, CAC, repeat purchase rate]
  8. Check campaigns against relevant requirements before launch, including [e.g. consumer protection, advertising standards, data privacy]
  9. Support the response to public-facing problems (outages, listing errors, customer complaints) by following our escalation plan, owned by [role]

What success looks like:

  1. First 30 days: you understand our customers, platform, product catalog, and current channels
  2. First 90 days: you own specific campaign or channel and reach target
  3. First 12 months: concrete outcome, e.g. a repeatable acquisition process, improved conversion on key product categories

What we are looking for:

  1. A balance of creative skills (copy, campaigns) and analytical skills (analytics, testing, reporting)
  2. Ability to explain technical and UX topics in plain language to customers and colleagues
  3. Clear, organized communication when handling several projects across teams
  4. Interest in how users move through a website, from discovering a product to completing an application or purchase
  5. Respect for working with a diverse team and across departments

What we offer:

  1. Salary: 10-13 (hiring target 11.5, ceiling 13)
  2. Benefits, working hours, learning budget
  3. Growth path: [e.g. Senior Marketing Specialist or Marketing Manager, with the criteria for promotion]

Security Engineer

Reference number: SEC02

About the role:
As our Security Engineer, you will design, implement, and maintain the security controls that protect our platform, networks, and customer data. You will work closely with engineering, UX, and IT so that new features ship securely and vulnerabilities are fixed quickly. You will report to the Head of Security / CISO (or CTO or Head of Engineering if none yet), with a dotted line to Engineering, the UX / Technical team, and IT.

What you will do:

  1. Design and maintain security controls for our systems and networks, including firewalls, network segmentation, and encryption of data in transit and at rest
  2. Review new features, product listing changes, and online application workflows with engineering and UX before release, and flag risks early
  3. Run regular security assessments, including vulnerability scans and [e.g. penetration tests, code or configuration reviews], and track findings through to remediation
  4. Monitor network and application traffic for suspicious activity using [tools, e.g. SIEM, IDS/IPS, WAF] and tune alerts to reduce noise
  5. Protect customer accounts and checkout flows against threats such as credential stuffing, account takeover, and bot abuse
  6. Lead the technical response to security incidents following our incident response plan, then write up what happened and what will change
  7. Write and maintain security policies and procedures, and help the team follow them in practice
  8. Keep our controls aligned with relevant requirements, such as [e.g. PCI DSS, privacy laws, industry standards]
  9. On-call: [state clearly whether there is an on-call rotation, how often, and how it is compensated]

What success looks like:

  1. First 30 days: you understand our architecture, data flows, and current security tooling, and have reviewed open vulnerabilities
  2. First 90 days: you own [specific area, e.g. vulnerability management or alert triage] and have reduced [e.g. time to remediate critical findings] to [target]
  3. First 12 months: [concrete outcome, e.g. a repeatable security review built into the release process, a tested incident response plan]

What we are looking for:

  1. Years of experience in security engineering, or a bachelor's degree in computer science, information technology, or a related field combined with strong hands-on projects
  2. Solid knowledge of network security, encryption, firewalls, and intrusion detection and prevention systems
  3. Experience with vulnerability management and security incident response
  4. Ability to explain risk in plain language to non-security colleagues and help them make good decisions
  5. Clear, organized communication when handling several priorities under pressure
  6. Nice to have: certifications such as CISSP, CISM, or CEH, and experience securing e-commerce or web applications

What we offer:

  1. Salary: 14-20 (hiring target 17, ceiling 20)
  2. Benefits, working hours, training and certification budget
  3. Growth path: [e.g. Senior Security Engineer or Security Lead, with the criteria for promotion]
