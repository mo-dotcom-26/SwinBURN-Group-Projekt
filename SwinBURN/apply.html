Our commitment to inclusion

SwinBURN is an online retail company with teams and applicants in both Vietnam and Australia. Because of this, we acknowledge the First Peoples of both countries on this page, which is where new team members first meet us. We encourage applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples in Australia and from people of all 54 ethnic groups in Vietnam, and we are working towards a workplace and an online platform that are accessible, culturally safe and welcoming to everyone.

Acknowledgement of Country (Australia)

SwinBURN acknowledges the Traditional Owners of the lands on which we live and work across Australia, and pays respect to Elders past and present. We recognise the continuing connection of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples to land, waters and community, and we are committed to reconciliation and to supporting First Nations communities.

Acknowledgement of Country (Vietnam)

We honour and celebrate the rich tapestry of Vietnam's Indigenous peoples, encompassing 54 distinct ethnic groups, including the Kinh, Tay, Thai, Muong, Co Tu, Ede, Khmer, Hmong, Cham and many others, whose ancestral stewardship of this land spans millennia.

We pay our deepest respect to our ancestors past, present and future for preserving Vietnam's cultures, history, timeless traditions and deep spiritual connection to its mountains, rivers, seas and forests.

We recognise their enduring heritage, resilience and invaluable contributions to the nation's diverse identity, committing to learn from and uplift their stories with reverence and gratitude.

These are our values as SwinBURN and we expect you to uphold them as our employees. We kindly request our employees-to-be, to educate themselves on these cultures to enhance their fit with our company.
